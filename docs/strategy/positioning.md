# Positioning recommendation

**Decision needed from:** owner (Nahid Tanjum). This is a recommendation. The site ships with the recommended line, and swapping it is a one-line change in `src/data/site.ts → positioning.headline`.

## The constraint that decides it

Fundex can't truthfully or safely lead with capital:
- There is no verifiable portfolio, fund, AUM or exit record (claims register A1–A25).
- Advertising investment, fund management or deal arrangement is a regulated activity in Australia unless the firm holds the right licence or exemption.

What Fundex *can* stand behind is **thinking plus doing**: helping founders and partners shape a strategy and then carry it out. In the benchmark (`docs/research/website-benchmark.md`), this is the studio/builder half of the market: Rainmaking, Hexa, Atomic, High Alpha Studio. It is not the VC half (Antler, Blackbird, Betatron).

## The three candidates

| Candidate | Says what Fundex does? | Truthful today? | Distinctive? | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **"Where Strategy Meets Execution"** | **Yes:** names both halves of the offer | **Yes:** describes a way of working, not a track record | Medium. The phrase is used in consulting, but it continues the current site's "intersection of capital, strategy, and execution" and the LinkedIn tagline "Capital. Strategy. Growth." | Low |
| "Building Businesses. Creating Opportunities." | Partly; implies Fundex builds businesses itself (a studio) | **Weak:** "building businesses" reads as a portfolio claim Fundex can't evidence yet | Low; generic | Medium. Invites "which businesses?" |
| "From Ambition to Execution" | Partly: describes the founder's journey, not Fundex's role | Yes | Medium | Low, but vaguer; the visitor still has to work out what Fundex sells |

## Recommendation: **"Where Strategy Meets Execution"**

Reasons:
1. **Clearest for a first-time visitor.** In five words it says what Fundex sells. The benchmark shows that unfamiliar brands need more explanatory headlines than established ones (compare PSL's 142-word homepage with Startmate's precise meta description).
2. **True on day one.** It describes a method, not results, so no claim needs evidence.
3. **Brand continuity.** It carries forward the existing "capital, strategy, and execution" idea and the LinkedIn tagline, without the regulated "capital" promise.
4. **Search fit.** "strategy" and "execution" match how Australian founders search for business strategy and growth help, more than "ambition" does.

"From ambition to execution" still has a use, as the **process framing** (the engagement steps on the Services page run from *ambition* to *execution*). The two lines then reinforce each other without competing for the H1.

## Supporting copy (as built)

- **H1:** Where strategy meets execution.
- **Sub-headline (refined in ChatGPT review round 1):** We help founders and growing businesses turn complex challenges into clear plans, practical solutions and measurable progress.
- **Primary CTA:** Start a conversation · **Secondary CTA:** See how we work
- **Who it's for:** founders and business leaders building or scaling a company, and organisations looking for capable commercial partners. Investor-facing wording ("well-prepared opportunities") was removed in review round 1 because it could imply investment introductions.
- **Proof strategy (no fabricated proof):** a transparent process, a named founder, verifiable company facts (legal entity, Melbourne, founded 2026), a scope statement ("what we don't do") and an honest Insights hub that starts empty.

## Voice

Plain, confident and specific. Australian English (organisation, programme → *program* per AU usage, "cheque"). No hype words ("disrupt", "world-class", "category-defining"), no superlatives that need evidence, and no numbers without a source.
